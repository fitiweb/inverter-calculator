How to Choose the Right Inverter Size for Your Home

The correct inverter size depends on the total electrical load you want to run at the same time. Appliances such as refrigerators, freezers, pumps and air conditioners can require additional starting power.

For this reason, simply adding appliance wattages is not always enough. A suitable safety margin should be included when selecting an inverter.

How to Calculate Battery Capacity

Battery capacity depends on your power consumption, required backup duration, battery voltage, battery chemistry and usable battery capacity.

A simplified calculation is:

Battery Energy = Load × Backup Time

The result can then be adjusted for battery efficiency, inverter losses and usable depth of discharge.

How Many Solar Panels Do I Need?

Solar panel sizing depends on your daily energy requirement and the amount of useful sunlight available. Panel wattage, system losses and solar conditions should also be considered.

The FITIWEBS calculator provides an initial estimate. A professional solar system design should verify the final panel configuration and MPPT voltage range.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the inverter size I need?

Add the running watts of the appliances you want to operate at the same time and allow additional capacity for starting or surge loads. The required inverter size also depends on power factor and appliance characteristics.

How many batteries do I need for my inverter?

Battery requirements depend on your total load, desired backup time, battery voltage, battery chemistry and usable depth of discharge.

How many solar panels do I need for my home?

Solar panel requirements depend on your daily energy consumption, panel wattage, available sunlight and system losses.

What battery is best for a solar inverter?

Lithium iron phosphate batteries are commonly preferred for many modern solar systems because they can provide high usable capacity and long cycle life. However, the battery should always be compatible with the selected inverter.

Can this calculator replace a professional solar design?

No. This tool provides an initial estimate. A professional installation should verify equipment specifications, MPPT limits, battery requirements, cable sizing, protection devices and site conditions.
